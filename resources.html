Learning Resources

Curated collection of books, courses, papers, and tools

📚 Textbooks

Probability Theory

  • "Probability Theory: The Logic of Science" by E.T. Jaynes
    Philosophical and rigorous approach to probability
  • "A First Course in Probability" by Sheldon Ross
    Excellent introductory text with many examples
  • "Probability and Random Processes" by Grimmett and Stirzaker
    Advanced treatment with rigorous proofs

Statistics

  • "All of Statistics" by Larry Wasserman
    Concise coverage of modern statistical methods
  • "Statistical Inference" by Casella & Berger
    Classic graduate-level text
  • "Computer Age Statistical Inference" by Efron & Hastie
    Modern perspective on statistics in the age of ML

Machine Learning & Deep Learning

  • "Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning" by Christopher Bishop
    Comprehensive ML text with Bayesian perspective
  • "Deep Learning" by Goodfellow, Bengio, and Courville
    The definitive deep learning textbook
  • "The Elements of Statistical Learning" by Hastie, Tibshirani, and Friedman
    Statistical perspective on machine learning
  • "Probabilistic Machine Learning" by Kevin Murphy (Books 1 & 2)
    Modern treatment of probabilistic ML methods
